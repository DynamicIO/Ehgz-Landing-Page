Why Ehgz

Designed like an heirloom, used like a habit.

We didn't set out to build another booking app. We set out to make the way Egypt eats, drinks and gathers feel effortless — and beautiful — on a phone.

A map, not a feed

Most apps drown you in scroll. Ehgz starts with the question that matters: where are you tonight? Our map and atmosphere filters answer it in two taps.

Made in Egypt

Every venue, every translation, every recommendation — local first.

No paid placements

Curation is editorial, not auctioned. Order is by quality, not by spend.

Filters that actually filter

"Romantic," "Rooftop & Views," "Live Entertainment" — atmosphere tags handpicked by our team. Combined with price tiers and city, you find the right place in seconds.

Your reservation history

Upcoming, completed, saved, total — at a glance from your profile.

Privacy by default

Your saved venues, profile, and reservation history live in your account — never sold, never shared. We collect the bare minimum needed to make Ehgz work.
