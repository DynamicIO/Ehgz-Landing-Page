Privacy Policy.

Last updated 1 May 2026. This page summarises how Ehgz handles your personal data, in clear language.

1. The short version

Your saved venues, profile, and reservation history live in your account — never sold, never shared with third-party advertisers. We collect only what we need to make Ehgz work.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: name, email, profile picture (optional).
  • Reservation data: venues booked, party size, date, status.
  • Device data: approximate location (with permission), device type, app version. Used to show nearby venues.
  • Diagnostics: anonymised crash reports to fix bugs.

3. How we use it

To deliver bookings, send reminder notifications you've opted into, recommend venues you may like, and improve the app. We do not run third-party advertising on Ehgz.

4. Sharing

When you book, the venue receives the minimum information they need: your name, party size, contact phone, and special requests. That's it.

5. Cookies on this website

This marketing site uses only essential cookies and an anonymised analytics pixel. No cross-site tracking, no advertising cookies.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data at any time from inside the app, or by emailing privacy@ehgz.app. We respond within 30 days.

7. Contact

Questions? Reach our Data Protection Officer at privacy@ehgz.app.
