FAQ

Everything you might ask, answered.

Is Ehgz really free? What's the catch?

There is no catch. Ehgz is free to download and we charge zero booking fees. You only pay the venue, exactly what you'd pay walking in. We work directly with our partner venues to keep it that way.

Which cities does Ehgz cover?

We're live across Cairo (including Zamalek, New Cairo, Sheikh Zayed and Maadi), Giza, Alexandria, the North Coast (Sahel) and El Gouna, with more cities being added every month.

How are venues curated?

Every venue on Ehgz is reviewed by our local team. We visit, vet and only list places we'd genuinely send our friends to. No paid placements, no algorithmic clutter.

Can I cancel a reservation?

Yes — most reservations can be cancelled or rescheduled directly from the app, free of charge. A few high-demand venues require advance notice; that's always shown upfront before you confirm.

Is there an Android app?

Ehgz is iOS-first today. An Android version is on the roadmap — sign up for our newsletter to be the first to know when it ships.

I own a venue. Can I get listed?

We'd love to hear from you. Head to our support page and submit a partnership request — our team usually replies within two business days.
